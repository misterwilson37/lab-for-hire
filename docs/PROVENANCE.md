# Provenance and licensing

Where everything came from, so nobody has to guess.

## Content
| Case | Data | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Movin' Molecules | Five points from Hanson's Lesson 5 "Math Connections" table (-20/550, 0/600, 40/700, 100/740, 120/750) | Questions are original. |
| The Cold Pack Challenge | **Sample data** (realistic trends, invented values) | Wording says "dissolves", not "reacts": dissolving is a physical process, though many middle-school curricula teach cold packs in the reactions unit. The coldest option is deliberately not the best choice (urea meets both rules and costs less). |
| Mission to Kestrel-b | **Real** water vapor-pressure data | Hanson's simplified worksheet key disagrees with real water (on real water 100 C at 4 atm is a liquid), so her worksheet Q1 would have two right answers here. Her worksheet's phase data table was never available. |
| Fog for Frankenstein | **Real** CO2 phase data, rounded, **log** pressure axis | Fills the gap that the unit test's nitrogen diagram uses a log axis and Lesson 5 never practiced one. |
| The Winter Bounce Test | **Sample data** following the gas law (8 psi at 20 C) | Two visuals: a text table with a "?" row and a bar graph. |
| Three teasers (cells, plant growth, medical design) | Advertisements only | Written to show what each piece of equipment unlocks; Hanson writes the real cases. |

**Not used, anywhere:** items from the state unit test, items from the STEMscopes worksheet (copyright Accelerate Learning, and test security), or Hanson's slides. They informed which skills and misconceptions to cover. The uploaded PDFs are deliberately not in the repo.
